# Internal Testing Release Notes - 2026-06-15

## English

Improved chapter learning with a clear 5-problem Free limit and options to
continue all 20 problems through a no-card 3-day Premium Preview or Premium.
Mock exams now stay within the learner's grade, wrong-note recommendations
stay in the same chapter, and several exam, lesson, and readability issues
were fixed.
