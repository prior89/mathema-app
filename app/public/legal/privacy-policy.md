This Privacy Policy describes how the **matheo** mobile application and
related services (the "Service", "we", "us") collect, use, share, and protect

Act, 2023 (DPDP)** as the primary statutory framework, and we additionally
align our practices with the **EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**
and **Google Play Families Policy** where they impose stricter requirements.

By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

> **At a glance.** We collect what we need to teach you mathematics — your

> sell your data. You can delete your account at any time from
> **Settings → Privacy → Delete account**, which permanently erases every

## 2. What we collect and why

A complete inventory is maintained in `PLAY_DATA_SAFETY.md` (in this
repository). Categories summarised here:

### 2.1 Information you provide

- **Account info** — email address, name, password (stored as a bcrypt
  hash), and your selected **NCERT class** (Class 7 through Class 12).
- **Profile info** — optional study goal (BOARD / JEE / SCHOOL / CATCHUP)
  and target grade.
- **Parent linkage** — if you create a parent account, your child's
  registered email (used only to map you to that student's data).
- **Tenant code** — if you register as a teacher with an institution code,
  the code identifies your school / academy.
- **Consent records** — your explicit yes/no to each optional purpose
  (analytics, marketing, AI-training), with an IP address and user agent
  captured at the time of consent for legal evidence (DPDP §6(1)).
- **Feedback** — any rating or comment you choose to submit in-app.

### 2.2 Information generated by your use of the Service

- **Practice attempts** — the questions you tried, the answers you chose,
  whether you were correct, how long it took, hint usage, optional
  confidence rating, optional step-by-step working.
- **Wrong notes** — the items you got wrong, your error pattern,
  spaced-repetition schedule, and the AI's diagnostic summary if you
  consented to AI analysis.
- **Mock exam results** — score, percentile, grade, time taken.
- **Concept lesson progress** — which lessons you started, completed steps,
  retrieval scores.
- **Daily activity** — minutes studied per day and the resulting heatmap +
  streak.
- **Mastery snapshots** — per-concept mastery scores derived from your
  attempts.
- **Recommendation log** — what the recommender suggested and whether you
  acted on it.
- **In-app interaction events** — page views, button taps, etc.
  (recorded **only if you grant `ANALYTICS` consent**).

### 2.3 Information automatically collected

- **Device identifiers** — push notification token (only after you grant
  the OS-level notification permission; marketing pushes additionally
  require active MARKETING consent in app settings), platform string
  (`android` / `ios` / `web`), app version.
- **Session id** — a random UUID generated by your browser/device to tie
  multiple requests together; resets when the app is closed.
- **Diagnostics** — crash logs (Sentry) and backend performance telemetry
  (Microsoft Azure Application Insights). Crash logs may include your user
  id so an engineer can reproduce the failure; you can suppress this by
  revoking `ANALYTICS` consent.

We do **not** collect precise GPS location, browsing history outside the app,
contact lists, or photos other than ones you explicitly upload to OCR a math
problem.

**Premium subscriptions (Google Play):** If you subscribe via the Android app,
Google Play processes payment. We receive an opaque purchase token and
subscription metadata (product, status, renewal dates) to grant Premium access.
We do **not** receive your card or bank details.

## 4. Who we share it with

We share only what is operationally necessary and only with vendors that
provide a contractual data-processing commitment (DPA). The full list is
maintained in `PLAY_DATA_SAFETY.md §2`.

- **OpenAI** — receives the text of math problems you attempt and your
  step-by-step working when you have granted `AI_TRAINING` consent. The

  moment you upload it, to extract math text via OCR.
- **Google (Cloud Identity)** — only when you choose **Sign in with
  Google**. We receive your email address, name, and Google `sub` id.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)** — receives your push token to
  deliver notifications.
- **Sentry** — receives crash stack traces.
- **Microsoft Azure (Application Insights)** — receives backend telemetry.

We disclose information when legally required (court order, subpoena, or
binding government request) and only the minimum compelled by the order.

We do **not** transfer your information to any other third party.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Active account data** — for as long as your account exists.
- **Deleted account** — permanently purged within **30 days** of your
  `DELETE /users/me` request. Backups containing the deletion window roll
  off within an additional **30 days**.
- **Crash logs (Sentry)** — 90 days, then auto-purged.
- **Backend audit logs** — 365 days (operational and legal recordkeeping).
- **Consent records** — retained for the longer of (a) account lifetime or
  (b) 3 years from revocation, whichever is later (DPDP evidence
  requirement).

## 7. Your rights

Under DPDP §11–§14 and GDPR Art. 15–22 you may:

1. **Access** the data we hold about you. Use **Settings → Privacy →
   Download my data**, which produces a JSON export of every record keyed to

3. **Withdraw consent** for optional purposes from **Settings → Privacy

   Privacy → Delete account**.
5. **Lodge a grievance** with our Grievance Officer (`VeraStudy01@gmail.com`)
   within 7 working days of an incident.
6. **Nominate** another person to exercise your rights in case of death or
   incapacity (DPDP §13). Contact us by email.

## 9. Security

See `PLAY_DATA_SAFETY.md §3` for the technical inventory. In summary:

- **Encryption in transit**: HTTPS only, no plaintext fallback.
- **Encryption at rest**: Azure-managed transparent encryption for the
  PostgreSQL database. Passwords are bcrypt-hashed (cost factor 10).
- **Access control**: production database access is restricted by firewall
  rules, managed credentials, and encrypted connections; admin operations
  are audited (`AdminAccessLog`).
- **Token security**: short-lived JWT access tokens + revocable refresh
  tokens (Redis-backed blacklist).
- **Incident response**: in the event of a personal-data breach we notify
  affected users and the Data Protection Board of India within **72 hours**
  per DPDP §8(6).

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your account has been
compromised, change your password immediately and contact us.
